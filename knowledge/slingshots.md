## [SL-001] What a gravity assist is
tags: slingshot, gravity assist, flyby, moving body, speed
level: intermediate

A planet or moon moves along its orbit, and when the probe passes it the pull bends the probe's path. Measured against the moving body, the probe leaves with the same speed it arrived with, only in a new direction. But the body itself is moving, so measured against the star the probe's speed and direction change. Passing behind a body that moves in your direction can speed you up, and passing in front of it can slow you down, all without burning fuel.

Try: look at the preview as you move the aim, and see how a pass near a moving body bends the path.

## [SL-002] Saving fuel with an assist
tags: save fuel, assist, efficient, slingshot, fuel budget, flyby
level: advanced

A well-timed flyby can replace a burn. If the path needs a change of speed or direction, check whether passing a body would give it for free before spending fuel. Keep the closest point above the body's surface, because a pass that gets too low becomes a crash. Assists depend on timing, because the body must be in the right place when you arrive. Fuel above one and a half times par is a sign to look for one, and later levels are built around them.

Try: compare a plan with a node to one that bends around a planet, and keep the cheaper one.
