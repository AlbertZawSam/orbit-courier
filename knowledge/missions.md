## [MI-001] The orbit delivery mission
tags: mission, deliver, orbit, band, hold, target, win
level: beginner

To deliver the cargo you must keep the probe inside a radius band around the target body for the full hold time without a break. Leaving the band resets the timer to zero, and the HUD shows your progress as a percentage. The mission line gives the target, the inner and outer radius of the band, and the hold time. A near-circular orbit inside the band is the safest way to finish, because an elongated orbit leaves the band on every lap.

Try: compare the band's middle radius with the circular speed there to judge the orbit you need.

## [MI-002] Stars and payouts
tags: stars, score, payout, par, credits, fuel
level: beginner

Completing a level earns one star. Using fuel at or below 125 percent of par earns two, and at or below par earns three, where par is the fuel used by the designer's stored solution. Payout adds a base amount, a bonus for fuel left in the tank, and a small bonus for finishing quickly. A crash or leaving the map pays nothing, but you can retry instantly. Many early levels have a par of zero, so a launch without burns is already a perfect score.

Try: after a win, retry with fewer or smaller burns to reach three stars.
