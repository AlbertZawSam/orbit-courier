## [MI-001] The orbit delivery mission
tags: mission, deliver, orbit, band, hold, target, win
level: beginner

To deliver the cargo you must keep the probe inside a radius band around the target body for the full hold time without a break. Leaving the band resets the timer to zero, and the HUD shows your progress as a percentage. The mission line gives the target, the inner and outer radius of the band, and the hold time. A near-circular orbit inside the band is the safest way to finish, because an elongated orbit leaves the band on every lap.

Try: compare the band's middle radius with the circular speed there to judge the orbit you need.

## [MI-002] Stars and payouts
tags: stars, score, payout, par, credits, fuel
level: beginner

Completing a level earns one star. Using fuel at or below 125 percent of par earns two, and at or below par earns three, where par is the fuel used by the designer's stored solution. Payout adds a base amount, a bonus for fuel left in the tank, and a small bonus for finishing quickly. A crash or leaving the map pays nothing, but you can retry instantly. Many early levels have a par of zero, so a launch without burns is already a perfect score.

Try: after a win, retry with fewer or smaller burns to reach three stars.

## [MI-003] Rendezvous with a station
tags: rendezvous, station, capture, relative speed, moving target, match
level: intermediate

A rendezvous succeeds when the probe is within the capture distance of the station and its speed relative to the station is below the limit. The station is moving, so what counts is the difference between your velocity and its velocity, not your speed against the sky. A fast pass through the capture area is not a capture: the probe keeps flying and you can try again. The HUD shows the gap and the relative speed while you fly. Arriving at the right time matters as much as the right path.

Try: aim to arrive alongside the station, then use a small burn to match its speed as the gap closes.

## [MI-004] Soft landing
tags: landing, soft landing, touchdown, surface, speed limit, crash
level: intermediate

To land you must touch the target's surface slower than the landing limit, measured relative to the surface, so a moving moon counts its own motion. Touching it any faster is a crash, and so is touching any other body at any speed. Gravity speeds you up as you fall, so slow down early: a retrograde burn well before touchdown works better than a hard burn at the last moment. The HUD shows your altitude and speed against the limit.

Try: add a retrograde node before the descent and check the preview's closing speed.

## [MI-005] Gate run
tags: gate, gates, order, fly through, ring, course
level: intermediate

A gate run asks you to fly through a series of gates in order. A gate is a short bar between two posts, and the probe passes it when its path crosses the bar. Crossing a later gate out of order does not count, but it does not end the attempt either: you can come back and cross it again once it is next. The next gate is shown in gold, finished gates in teal, and the HUD tells you which gate is next. Passing around the end of a bar does not count.

Try: place nodes so the preview line crosses each bar in turn, in order.
