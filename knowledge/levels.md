## [LV-001] Level 1: First Light
tags: level 1, first light, kepi, capture, l1
levels: L1
level: beginner

Reach Kepi and stay close to it for 4 seconds, anywhere between 0.8 and 2.5 units from it. Kepi has mu 14, so it can catch a probe that arrives slowly enough. A launch at about 82 degrees with power near 5.5 works: aim a little ahead of Kepi, not straight at it, because it moves while you fly. You do not need any burns, so the par fuel is zero. If the probe flies past, lower the power slightly.

Try: start from 82 degrees at about 80 percent power, then adjust with Shift and the arrow keys.

## [LV-002] Level 2: Falling Around
tags: level 2, falling around, kepi, orbit, l2
levels: L2
level: beginner

Kepi is heavier here (mu 30) and starts on a different side, so it holds on to the probe more strongly. The goal is to stay between 0.8 and 3.5 units of Kepi for 6 seconds. A launch near 246 degrees at power about 5.4 works with no burns. Because the pull is stronger, a probe that arrives too fast can still bend into a loop, so the path often curves around Kepi on the preview. Par fuel is zero.

Try: aim near 246 degrees at about 77 percent power and check that the preview curls around Kepi.

## [LV-003] Level 3: Burn Nodes
tags: level 3, burn nodes, retrograde, hold, kepi, l3
levels: L3
level: intermediate

The hold time here is 12 seconds, between 0.8 and 3.5 units of Kepi, which a launch alone cannot manage: the probe swings past and leaves. Launch near 156 degrees at power about 6.25, then add one node about 5.6 seconds into the flight, close to the moment of closest approach, with a small retrograde burn of about 0.3. Any time between about 4.8 and 6.4 seconds works. Par fuel is 0.3.

Try: add the node by clicking the path near the closest approach, then drag the brass handle backward.
