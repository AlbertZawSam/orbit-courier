## [TR-001] Launch angle and power
tags: launch, angle, power, aim, speed, practice
level: beginner

The launch angle chooses the direction and the power sets the speed relative to the planet. Power up to 7 is allowed, and escape speed from Home's pad is about 6.9. In the early levels, powers between roughly 5 and 6.3 reach the moon with a manageable speed. Small changes matter: a power change of 0.03 can shift where you arrive by a lot, so make small adjustments and check the preview. Angle decides where the path crosses the moon's orbit.

Try: set the angle first so the path crosses the target's orbit, then tune the power.

## [TR-002] Making fine adjustments
tags: fine, adjust, precision, shift, keys, tune
level: beginner

Dragging is good for rough aiming and the arrow keys are good for fine control. Holding Shift makes the steps much smaller: about 0.01 radians for the angle and about 0.02 speed units for the power. Change only one thing at a time, then read the preview. If a path almost works, try the smallest step in each direction and see which way it improves. Slow motion is available in flight for node handles.

Try: when the path is close, switch from dragging to Shift plus the arrow keys.
